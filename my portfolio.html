Vehicle Count Prediction System

To estimate traffic flow for smarter city management.

Managing traffic in cities is hard when we don’t have accurate vehicle count data. Traditional counting methods take a lot of effort, cost more, and can be wrong. Using machine learning, we can predict vehicle counts faster, cheaper, and more accurately. The idea is to use past traffic data, like time, day, and weather, to predict how many vehicles will pass a certain spot. This helps traffic managers plan better and reduce congestion.
